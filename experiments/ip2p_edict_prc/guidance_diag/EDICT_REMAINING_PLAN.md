# Remaining EDICT photos

`EDICT/experiment_images` has 21 photos. Six already have this receiver: the spaniel, the greyhound, the cat, the church, the fountain, and the lake. This round is the other 15.

Same four sender settings: text 7.5 or 3.0, image 1.5 or 1.0. Two instructions per photo, one appearance change and one added object. Same PRC key and saved noise. The receivers see the edited PNG, the key, and the sampler settings. One also sees the instruction. Neither sees the source photo.

LPIPS against the source is recorded for each edit. In the canvas, a larger dot means a smaller LPIPS, so the edit stayed closer to the original photo.
